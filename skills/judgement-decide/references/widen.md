# Widen

Criteria first, options second. Anything you want to add to the options goes in here, before the
screening starts.

## Criteria

1. Ask what a good choice would have to do. Draw out three to six.
2. Mark each `must` or `want`. A must is something they would walk away over.
3. Put them in order by asking, for neighbours, "if you could only have one?"
4. Say what a 1 and a 5 look like for each.
5. Stamp `fixedAt`. From here the criteria do not change to suit an option. If a new one turns out
   to matter, add it with a new time, say why in an event, and rate every option on it.

## Options

1. Ask for theirs first.
2. Then add yours, marked `from: "model"`. Reach for options that differ in kind:
   - do nothing yet, and what that costs
   - the smallest step that teaches something
   - one that fails in a different way from the favourite
   - one that drops the dearest criterion to win on the rest
   - one that a person affected by it would choose
3. Aim for four to six. With two, ask what else.
4. For each, write what must be true for it to work.

A choice that "does both" is welcome once it says what each half gives up and what would show it
failing. Without that it is two options pretending to be one.

Once screening starts, add a new option only by comparing it with the ones being kept.
