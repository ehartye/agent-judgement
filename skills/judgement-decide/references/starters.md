# Starters

A head start for common kinds of decision. Use the prompts as questions to ask, never as answers.
Their words replace these.

## Joining two organisations

- **Question shape:** What has to be one by when, and what stays two?
- **Often missed:** the people who run the thing being absorbed; customers in the overlap; who can
  say stop.
- **Criteria to offer:** customers kept; key people kept; one-off cost; date the main system is
  shared; what can be reversed.
- **Usual tension:** speed against keeping what was worth buying. Try separating by place or time.
- **Find out first:** which of the promised gains has evidence, and which is an assumption.
- **Early sign:** people who were named as essential start leaving.

## Which technology or AI efforts to back

- **Question shape:** Which few, in what order, and what do we stop?
- **Often missed:** the person whose daily work changes; who maintains it in year two.
- **Criteria to offer:** value someone can measure; time to first use; data in hand; cost to run;
  skill kept in house.
- **Usual tension:** spreading bets against finishing one. Try separating by scale: small tests many,
  full builds few.
- **Find out first:** one case where the same kind of effort paid off, and one where it did not.
- **Early sign:** use drops off after the first month.

## What to say and to whom

- **Question shape:** Who must understand what, to do which thing?
- **Often missed:** the person who decides is seldom the person who reads it.
- **Criteria to offer:** understood by the buyer; true and provable; different from the alternatives;
  cheap to test.
- **Usual tension:** two audiences needing different messages. Try separating by place: two tracks.
- **Find out first:** what five real people say it does after one minute.
- **Early sign:** people praise it and do nothing.

## A programme that is stuck

- **Question shape:** What would unblock it that the parties could each say yes to?
- **Often missed:** who gains from the delay; what nobody has authority to do.
- **Criteria to offer:** each party's must; visible progress in six months; what it costs to stop;
  load on the people carrying it.
- **Usual tension:** everyone at the table against anything getting done. Try separating by role: few
  decide, many are heard.
- **Find out first:** the last time these parties agreed on something, and why.
- **Early sign:** meetings grow and decisions do not.

## Choosing among new ventures or ideas

- **Question shape:** Which do we test next, and what would make us stop?
- **Often missed:** the cost of the attention, not only the money.
- **Criteria to offer:** someone will pay; we can do it; we learn fast; we can afford to be wrong.
- **Usual tension:** the biggest prize against the surest one. Try separating by time: test the big
  one, live on the sure one.
- **Find out first:** the cheapest test that could kill each idea. Run those before choosing.
- **Early sign:** the test gets changed to keep the idea alive.

## Rolling something out across places or cultures

- **Question shape:** What must be the same everywhere, and what should each place decide?
- **Often missed:** who will lose face; how bad news travels in each place; the words themselves.
- **Criteria to offer:** works in the hardest place; local owner named; message survives translation;
  can pause one place without stopping the rest.
- **Usual tension:** one standard against local fit. Try separating by condition: fixed outcome, free
  method.
- **Find out first:** the problem as the hardest place describes it. It may be a question of who
  decides rather than of the system.
- **Early sign:** agreement in the meeting and silence after it.

## Choosing a design or an approach

- **Question shape:** Which approach gets us what matters most with what we have?
- **Often missed:** what the team can build well; what breaks first; what the choice rules out later.
- **Criteria to offer:** does the one job that matters; time to a working version; what fails and how
  badly; fits the skill and tools on hand; can be changed late.
- **Usual tension:** best possible against surely finished. Try separating by time: a simple version
  that works, then a better one.
- **Find out first:** a rough test of the riskiest part, this week.
- **Early sign:** the working version keeps moving back.
