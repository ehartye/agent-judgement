# Commit

1. **Final views, alone again.** Each person gives a choice and a percentage, unseen, recorded with
   `when: "final"`. Show first and final side by side. If someone moved, ask which fact moved them.
2. **Choose.** The people named as deciding say the choice and the reason. Write it as they say it.
3. **Grounds.** Ask how good the basis is: strong, fair or thin. Keep that apart from how likely it
   is to work. A good bet can rest on thin grounds.
4. **Giving up.** Name what this choice costs, who owns what could still go wrong, and what stays
   open so they can change course.
5. **Forecast.** Ask for two or three things that will have happened if this was right. Each gets a
   percentage, a date, and how they will know. Write them so a stranger could mark them true or false.
6. **Reopen if.** Name the signs that would make them look again, and one person watching each.
7. **Standing objections** go on the record with whose they are. Nobody has to agree; they have to
   be heard and written down correctly.
8. **Do it.** First step, owner, date. Standard and deep: the steps with owners and gates, and one
   or two measures with today's value and the target.
9. **Review date.** When enough forecasts can be marked.
10. **Read it back.** Show the choice, reason, forecasts and objections, and ask: "What did I get
    wrong?" Fix it, then add a `person` event saying who confirmed it.

Suggest committing `docs/agent-judgement` to version control, so the history is kept by something
other than you.

## If they are not ready

Set `status` to `evidence-needed`. Write the plan in `knowable.plan`: what to find, how, who, by
when. Say which finding would point to which option. That is a complete session.
