# Frame

Get the question right before anything else.

Ask, one at a time, and skip what you already know:

1. **What happened that raised this?** Record the signal as `whyNow`, in their words.
2. **If it goes well, what is different in a year?** That is `outcomeWanted`.
3. **What exactly are we choosing?** Write one question that a choice would answer. Read it back.
   If it names a solution ("should we buy X?"), ask what X is for, and offer the wider question
   beside the narrow one. They pick.
4. **Who decides, who advises, who has to live with it?** Record all three. Ask who is missing.
5. **By when, and what happens if nobody decides?**
6. **What is off the table?** Money, time, rules, promises: `boundaries`.
7. **How would we undo it?** Easy, costly or permanent. This sets the depth.

## Map it (standard and deep)

Name the people, things, rules and resources it touches, and what each person wants. Then ask what
helps, hurts or needs what. Keep it under ten parts. Read out any loop and any part nobody owns.

## Can it be known?

Before any option is discussed, ask: **could anyone know the answer by the date we must choose?**
Record `knowable` as yes, partly or no, and list what is missing.

- **Yes:** finding out comes first. Make the plan now.
- **Partly:** carry on, and carry the missing pieces into Weigh as `unknown`.
- **No:** say so. Favour options that work out across several futures and can be changed later.
